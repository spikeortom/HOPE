## 🌍 Why this project exists

For decades, global supply chains have kept shifting — where things are made, and by whom. Today, more and more companies are moving beyond *selling products* — they are **building factories, acquiring brands and setting up operations** abroad. For example:

- CATL's batteries go into Tesla, BMW and Mercedes-Benz
- Fuyao Glass supplies carmakers all over the world
- BYD builds EVs in a joint venture with Toyota
- Midea acquired Toshiba's home-appliance business and Germany's KUKA
- State Grid holds equity in power grids in Portugal, Italy and Greece

We want to record this journey with open, verifiable data.

**If you are job-hunting**, think of this as a "company strength" reference: a company's overseas customers, plants and global footprint say a lot about its industry position, resilience, and the international roles it may offer. Check where your target company operates and who it works with — it makes comparisons much easier.

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Where does the data come from? Is it accurate?

A: This project was created with AI. Data is compiled from public sources (company websites, annual reports, reputable media), and most entries carry a source link you can verify yourself. For reference only — for precise figures, consult official sources.

### Q: Does it include overseas factories?

A: Yes! The "overseas-factory" records include location, products, capacity and construction status — currently the largest relationship type in the dataset.

### Q: Can I contribute?

A: Absolutely — additional relationships, corrections and new companies are all welcome.
